Exclusive executive residence in the heart of the Eixample district
In one of the most emblematic areas of Barcelona's Eixample is located this exceptional 441 m² home, designed to live and work in balance. A home where spaciousness, natural light and serenity define each space, offering an unusual residential experience in the city.
A home designed for unhurried living, with privacy, generous proportions and a calm that is hard to find in Barcelona.
Large day area - Grand Living Area
Spacious and bright living-dining room that marks the beginning of the house. An open and welcoming space, ideal for day-to-day living and for sharing moments, where the feeling of spaciousness and comfort is always present.
Master Retreat - Private retreat
The master suite offers an intimate and quiet environment, with a large dressing room and an exclusive wellness area equipped with sauna, chromotherapy and aromatherapy, designed for rest and disconnection.
Executive Wing - Life and work in harmony
The house incorporates an independent Executive Wing, with its own access and courtesy toilet. A versatile and discreet space, ideal as a professional office, study or work area separated from the private area.
Kitchen and pantry
Functional and luminous kitchen and pantry, perfectly integrated in the house, designed to provide order, fluidity and comfort in daily use.
Multipurpose private suite
Independent space with multiple possibilities: auxiliary bedroom, study, private living room or creative space. It has natural light, its own bathroom and total independence.
Singular distribution
The house is organized from an original diamond-shaped structure, which allows a fluid circulation between rooms, favors the entry of light and conveys a constant sense of balance and spaciousness.
The building
Located in a stately building of great architectural value, recognized with a mention in the Annual Competition of Artistic Buildings of Barcelona in 1919, elegantly integrated into the urban fabric of the Eixample.

The Dreta de L'Eixample starts at Plaça de Catalunya and its central axis revolves around Passeig de Gràcia and the Cuadrat d'Or. It is delimited from the busy Calle Balmes to the renovated Passeig de Sant Joan and from the commercial Ronda Sant Pere to the convenient Avinguda Diagonal. It is one of the most expensive and exclusive neighborhoods in Barcelona, along with Pedralbes and Sarrià-Sant Gervasi.

It is impossible not to refer to the luxury of Paseo de Gràcia, with the boutiques of the most exclusive brands in the world, or the stores of La Rambla de Catalunya. However, the rest of its streets are just as interesting, if not more so. They were home to part of the Catalan textile bourgeoisie of the early twentieth century, which has left its mark on the spectacular royal estates and modernist houses designed by the most famous architects of the time, such as La Pedrera or Casa Batlló by the great Antoni Gaudí.

This is the best neighborhood if you want to admire and live in the noble area of the fascinating Catalan modernism, reside in one of its characteristic royal estates, and live in perfect balance with the vibrant and luxurious commercial life. It is ideal if you want to walk every day through its wide and orderly streets, with easy transport connections, have all kinds of services and facilities including charter or private schools, and enjoy the most chic, cultural and cosmopolitan atmosphere of Barcelona.
